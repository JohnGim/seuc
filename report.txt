*Report on Sport Resort Fiesch -- a potential conference venue in the Swiss Alps (this report was not written with the help of AI)*

_Initial takeaway_
This venue is larger, of a higher quality on every level, and more expensive. But it might well be worth it.

_Total capacity:_ 941 beds (additional capacity is available in the village, which is a 5-minute walk away, and in Brig, which is 20 minutes away by a lovely mountain train/or by car)

_Accommodation:_ Most rooms are ensuite and in good condition. Some 10 bed-rooms that are en-suite, but they would allocate us extra beds understanding that we would want to put at most 7-8 in a room. The two buildings with larger rooms have additional toilet and showering facilities and the toilets and showers are in separate compartments in the rooms. Most of the buildings have 2, 4, 6 beds, with a combination of single and bunk beds. All rooms have designated storage for each person's belongings.

_Catering:_ Full board plus snacks, with food served buffet style. They have a set menu, but we can work with them on it. It generally has three courses. They always provide a normal option and a vegetarian option and can accommodate other food allergies. Water is free and coffee is free in the morning and afternoon. All other drinks can be purchased by the attendees in the cafeteria; the cafeteria can handle 500+ people at one time, and their proposal is to have 150 line up every 15 minutes untill everyone has food. This means that meals would take approximately 1.5 hours in the schedule.

_Group meeting rooms, serving ones' meeting rooms:_ one room that can handle 150+, other rooms can comfortably handle 42-50 groups and all tables and chairs are provided.

_Recreational facilities:_ extensive outdoor and indoor offerings, as well as recreation rooms in the accommodation units with ping-pong tables; hiking and gondola options available in the nearby village. Lots of indoor and outdoor common spaces for people to hang out.

_Medical provision:_ two doctors are in the village and the emergency/hospital facilities are 30 minutes away I
in Visp. There is an ambulance station in the village, which allows for a quicker transit to Visp.

_Main meeting room:_ The gym can be partitioned into three parts, with two parts being for the meeting space (seating for 750+) and one part being for recreation. They will provide carpet and chairs. Their AV system is simple, but we can bring our own.

_Transportation:_ There are four main options, with the Milan option having three variants:
- *Option 1 - Zürich* - Fly into Zürich and take the train to Fiesch-Feriendorf via Bern and Brig (return train ticket will be 220 €, plus flight); renting a car and driving is also possible.
- *Option 2 - Basel* - Fly into Basel and take the train from Basel SBB via Brig to Fiesch-Feriendorf (return train ticket cost would be 210-215 €, plus flight cost); renting a car and driving is also possible.
- *Option 3 - Geneva* - Fly into Geneva and take a train via Brig to Fiesch-Feriendorf (return train ticket cost would be 175 €, plus flight cost); renting a car and driving is also possible.
- *Option 4 - Milan:*
- _Variant 1_ Fly into Milan and take the train from MSP Airport (3 hours and 30 minutes), Bergamo Airport (4 hours and 40 minutes) to Fiesch-Feriendorf. *Cost:* 60-150 €, plus flight cost; renting a car and driving is also possible.
- _Variant 2_ Fly into Milan and take a conference-arranged coach from MSP Airport (2 hours and 30 minutes) or Bergamo Airport (3 hours and 10 minutes) to Fiesch-Feriendorf via the Simplon Pass through the Alps. *Cost:* 60 € return, plus flight (60-90 € return)
- _Variant 3_ Fly into Milan and take a conference-arranged coach from MSP Airport to Fiesch-Feriendorf via the Great St. Bernard Pass if the Simplon Pass is snowed over (4 hours and 30 minutes). *Cost:* 60 € return, plus flight (60-90 € return)

_Booking Conditions:_ It is possible to reserve this venue for March 24-26, 2028 or April 28-30, 2028. We can first reserve the venue and then a deposit of 50% of the number we book for will be due prior to the event, and then the remaining 50% will be due 30 days after the event via invoice. Exclusive use is possible with 700 people, but they told us that if we are 500-600, then they will prefer to just have us to deal with and any other groups would be quite small and would not conflict with our use of the facilities, apart from dining together.

 _Total registration cost:_ Based on the quote they have provided, we would probably charge 180 € for a weekend. This would not include the shuttle from Milan. If we do three nights, then we would look at 245 €. Given the suitability of the venue and the investment in time and money to get there, this may well be the right time to expand the conference from two to three nights. 

_Closing thoughts:_ The venue is a significant upgrade from De Hoof and the Kasteel at Baarlo. The accommodation, catering, recreational facilities, small group meeting spaces, and large meeting space are purpose built and it shows. The facilities leave adequate capacity for the conference to grow.
